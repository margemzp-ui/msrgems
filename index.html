Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

Where are my recordings stored?+

Completed recordings and snapshots are saved to the Facescreen Recorder folder inside your browser's configured Downloads directory.

Does Facescreen upload or track anything?+

No. Recording and processing happen locally in Chrome. Facescreen does not require an account and contains no analytics or cloud-upload service.

What recording formats are supported?+

Chrome currently creates WebM video files through its built-in MediaRecorder API. Snapshots are saved as PNG files.

Can I record system audio?+

Yes, when Chrome and your operating system support audio for the source you select. Enable “Share audio” in Chrome's source picker when it is available.
